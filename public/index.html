What it does

It stays tucked into the notch until you need it. Sasquatches are good at that.

  • Hover to open

    Point at the notch and the island opens. Click inside and it stays open until you're done.

  • Calendar and reminders

    Today's events at a glance. Reminders due today sit just below, and you can tick them off right there.

  • Keep Awake

    Stop your Mac from dozing off, for anything from 15 minutes to 8 hours, or until you turn it off.

  • Music visualizer

    See what's playing and control it. Little bars bounce along with the song.

  • Charging and Bluetooth pop-ups

    Plug in your charger or connect your headphones, and a small pop-up shows the battery level.

  • Focus, camera and mic notices

    A quick heads-up when Focus turns on or off, or when an app starts using your camera or microphone.

  • Lock screen weather

    Check the weather before you even unlock. While your Mac charges, the lock screen shows the battery too.

  • Timers

    Start a timer from the notch and keep an eye on it while you work.

  • Shelf

    Drop files on the notch to park them for a bit. Drag them back out when you need them.

  • Color picker

    Pick any color on your screen and copy its code.

Check your Mac

Click the Apple menu in the top-left corner and choose About This Mac. Next to Chip, it should say Apple M1 or newer. SasNotch also needs macOS 14 Sonoma or later.

Questions

Is it free?

Yes. There's no trial, no ads and no account. SasNotch is free and open source.

How do I use it?

Point at the notch to open the island. Right-click the sasquatch in the menu bar for quick controls, and use Settings › Features to pick your tools. The 1-minute guide shows all three.

Is it safe?

The code is public on GitHub, so anyone can read exactly what SasNotch does. Every update is signed, and SasNotch checks that signature before it installs anything.

How do I update it?

You don't have to do anything. SasNotch checks for a new version once a day and asks before it installs one. You can also click its menu bar icon and choose Check for Updates.

How do I uninstall it?

Click the SasNotch icon in the menu bar and choose Quit. Then drag SasNotch from your Applications folder to the Trash.
